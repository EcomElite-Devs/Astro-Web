Questions people ask first

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

We can work with an approximate time. Yash can narrow it down using key life events, and he will tell you how much confidence to place in each part of the reading.

Can we meet online?

Yes. Sessions run on video call or phone, and you can also visit the Delhi studio. You receive the same written summary either way.

Will you recommend expensive rituals?

No. Remedies are optional, simple and explained in plain language. You will never be pushed into a costly ritual you do not need.

How accurate are the predictions?

Astrology is a belief-based practice and results vary. A chart shows tendencies and timing, so each reading focuses on practical steps rather than fixed outcomes.
